Discover Arsalan fabrics signature formal edit. An exquisite curation of stitched ready-to-wear and luxury unstitched ensembles. Meticulously designed raw silk panels, hand-embellished chiffon dupattas, and pure organza craftsmanship tailored for modern celebrations in Pakistan.

The Craft Narrative

Every silhouette is built with meticulous Pakistani heritage weaving. We pair pure Resham threads with glass seed-beads and delicate tilla work, honoring classical atelier methods while preserving a lightweight, contemporary wearability.
